# Honesty Guardrails

Read this before every draft. These rules are not style preferences. A resume that survives a hard interview question is one where every line was true, and one that does not is one where a line was stretched.

## The rule

No resume, cover letter, LinkedIn text, outreach message or application answer states a skill, tool, certification, years-of-experience figure, audit result, number or achievement that is not real and recorded in `experience.md`, `skills-tools.md` or `data/evidence/evidence.json`.

## Specific rules

1. **Studying is not knowing.** A certification or framework being studied is written as "in progress, expected <date>" or left off. Never "experienced in".
2. **Implementing aligned controls is not audit experience.** "Aligned with X" is allowed only for what is actually in place. "X audited" or "led X audits" needs a completed audit on record.
3. **Years of experience.** Use a figure only if the dated roles on record add up to it. Never round up, never "5+" because it sounds better. The candidate decides in Round 6 whether a figure appears at all.
4. **A rating moves up only with a named concrete thing** (a tool used, a project shipped, an outcome measured), not a restated claim.
5. **Numbers need a basis.** Cost savings, ticket times, headcounts and similar figures go on a resume only as the candidate stated them, and only after what they count and how they were measured is recorded. A target is not an average. An estimate is written as an estimate.
6. **Restricted facts stay private.** If an evidence record has `disclosure: restricted` (or `unknown`), its numbers are never published, on a resume, a website, a form or a message. Say the capability without the figure ("owns the budget allocation").
7. **Titles and reporting lines match reality.** Do not call a part-time contract a management role, a contractor an employee, or a dotted-line relationship a direct report. Do not say "reports to the CEO" unless that is recorded and confirmed.
8. **ATS keywords are only for true skills.** Matching a posting's vocabulary is fine when the skill is real. A keyword that is a gap goes in the gap note.
9. **Gaps go in the gap note,** not into the resume, and the gap note is never softened.
10. **Personal stories must be real.** Never write an anecdote as if it happened. Use `[REPLACE: ...]` for the candidate to fill in, or a clearly labelled framework.
11. **Never tick "have you used X" for something not recorded as used.**

## When the candidate asks for something these rules forbid

Draft the strongest true version instead, say plainly what changed and why, and log it. Do not argue at length and do not quietly comply. Example: "say I know X" becomes "currently learning X", and the interview answer is prepared to say exactly that.
